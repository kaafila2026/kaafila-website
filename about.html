The Theme

Taana Baana

The Timelessness of Humanity

Taana Baana — taana, the warp; baana, the weft — names the threads that cross to make cloth. Kaafila 2026 borrows that word for a simple idea: across every era and every culture, certain threads in us never change.

Through music, dance, visual arts, and theatre, this year's Kaafila traces what stays constant in the human spirit — emotion, storytelling, rhythm, and imagination — even as the forms that carry them keep changing.

Four Art Forms

The Threads

  • Music
  • Dance
  • Visual Arts
  • Theatre

Each art form offers students a unique way to interpret and express the theme of Taana Baana — a different thread, the same cloth.

Our Purpose

Why Kaafila

  • Encourage creative expression across every art form, not just the ones students already feel confident in.
  • Build confidence through performance and display, in front of an audience that is rooting for them.
  • Celebrate collaboration and individual talent side by side — group acts and solo work both have a place.
  • Connect students with the timeless nature of art and humanity, across generations and forms.
