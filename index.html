About Kaafila

Kaafila is Shiv Nadar School's annual celebration of the arts. It brings together students across different forms of creative expression to explore, create, and perform — a caravan (the word's literal meaning) of makers moving together toward one festival.

Every performer, artist, and photographer at Kaafila is, in a small way, weaving their own thread into this pattern. The festival doesn't ask any one art form to speak for all of them — it asks each to bring its own weave, so the whole cloth is stronger for the difference.
